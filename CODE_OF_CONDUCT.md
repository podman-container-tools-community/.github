# Code of Conduct

This community follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

We want everyone to feel welcome. Be respectful, assume good intent, and keep discussions focused on
the work.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainers by reaching out to [@jkroepke](https://github.com/jkroepke) on GitHub.
All reports are handled confidentially.
